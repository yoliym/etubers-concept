Etubers Visual DNA Summary

1. Worth Preserving (The Core DNA)

  • Creator Centrality: Mikiyas is the definitive focal point, looking directly at the viewer to establish authority and trust.
  • Layered Depth: Text frequently passes behind the creator, creating a 3D, high-production feel.
  • Bold Display Sans-Serif: Typography is massive, tightly tracked, and ultra-bold. Serif fonts are entirely absent.
  • High Contrast: Deep backgrounds (navy, charcoal, rich purple) contrasted with pure white or bright accent text.
  • Action Orientation: Visuals imply results (charts, dashboards, software icons).

2. Elements to Upgrade for a Premium Website

  • Refine the "Clickbait" Edge: Reduce drop shadows on text, oversized red strokes, and literal luxury imagery (cars/Dubai) in favor of sleek, structural premium design.
  • Backgrounds: Move from flat gradients to rich, subtle textures or sophisticated color blocks (like deep charcoal and warm ivory).
  • UI Elements: Replace literal dashboard screenshots with refined, stylized graphic representations of data or progress.
